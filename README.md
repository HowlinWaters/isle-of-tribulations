# Isle of Tribulations
## Synopsis
You are suddenly washed ashore on an island without any memory. As you wake up, you see four buildings of different colors standing in front of your eyes. Surrounded by an ocean of water, you have no choice but to enter in one of them.

## Description
Isle of Tribulations is an action-adventure game that revolves around puzzle-solving, combat, and exploration. Each dungeon contains a unique set of challenges the player must conquer to successfully win against its obstacles. Players must be able to master all the abilities they possess in order to bypass the many trials brought upon them. At the end, a player must fight the dungeon keeper, so they can obtain a hidden artifact that can only generate many questions with no answers to follow.

## Controls
- Movement: WASD / Arrow keys
- Camera movement: Q and E
- Attack: Enter/Return
- Pick up: Z

## Content
As of now, only one dungeon is fully complete and playable for players to play in.
